Detection Pipeline

From upload to verdict — every step of the forensic workflow.

1

Upload & Validate

Drag-and-drop or browse. File type and size validation runs on the client before any processing begins.

2

Preprocessing

Images are decoded and downscaled to 256×256. Videos are sampled into frames at uniform intervals.

3

Feature Extraction

DCT frequency energy, sensor-noise residual, color-channel consistency, and edge-sharpness distribution are computed per frame.

4

Ensemble Scoring

A weighted ensemble combines all signals through a calibrated sigmoid into a 0–1 probability of AI generation.

5

Verdict & Report

Results are persisted with full metrics, a PDF report is generated with QR verification, and the record enters your history.

6

Audit & History

Every action is logged. Search, filter, and re-download reports anytime from your dashboard.

About

Forensic-grade media authentication for the synthetic-media era.

The AI-Driven Deepfake Detection System is a forensic platform designed for cyber-security analysts, journalists, and legal teams who need to verify the authenticity of digital media. It combines multiple signal-processing heuristics inspired by the deepfake-detection literature — frequency-domain analysis, sensor-noise estimation, color consistency, and edge profiling — into a single calibrated score. Every analysis is logged, every report is certified with a unique ID and QR code, and the entire system is built on a modular architecture ready for cloud deployment.
